The Diligent Shadow·Tokyo Prototype 2026·Source

A switch stands on a white plinth in front of a wall. On the wall is the shadow of a woman sitting in a chair. When you turn the switch on, a lamp lights the wall, and she gets up, walks over to the switch's shadow and kicks it, and the switch turns off.

If you hold the switch on, her kicks fail. She leans on it, tries again, and eventually gives up and sulks, though now and then she runs at it once more. If you turn it off yourself, she walks back to her chair in the dark, and if you flip it back and forth too quickly, she loses her temper.

This is a web re-creation of Koya Endo's installation, shown at Tokyo Prototype 2026. Everything on the wall is a real shadow, cast by a single lamp from 3D objects that are never drawn.
